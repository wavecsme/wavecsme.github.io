G Ads MCP

G Ads MCP is a personal, single-user tool that connects an AI assistant to the owner's own Google Ads account through the Google Ads API. It is used to read reports, research keywords, and manage the owner's own campaigns.

The app is not offered to the public and is used only by its owner. It accesses only Google Ads accounts that the owner already has permission to manage.
